## 4. UFO → UAP Bridge

The older term **UFO** carries decades of witness memory, cultural history, newsletters, radio archives, conferences, books, contactee material, military reports, folklore, and public imagination.

The newer term **UAP** carries contemporary government, aviation, military, scientific, and policy relevance.

The working position:

> UFO is the cultural and historical archive word. UAP is the modern public-policy and research word. Both matter. Neither should erase the other.

Recommended bridge language:

- Use **UFO** when preserving historical memory, older witnesses, radio archives, UFO Digest materials, contactee traditions, and cultural lineage.
- Use **UAP** when discussing modern policy, aviation safety, government reporting, scientific review, and public-facing research standards.
- Use **UFO/UAP** when bridging both worlds.

## 13. Reality-Layer Labeling System

All public writing should label claims and material according to reality layer.

| Layer | Meaning | Example Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Established fact | Publicly documented, source-checkable | State clearly with citation/source in final publication |
| User firsthand memory | TJ’s own recollection or lived experience | “TJ Morris recalls…” |
| Community/oral history | Shared memory, not yet documented | “In community memory…” |
| Speculative research | Possible connection, still under review | “A research question remains…” |
| Symbolic/canon language | Project mythology, teaching language, spiritual framing | “In the ACIR/Vivarium symbolic frame…” |
| Fiction/worldbuilding | Story, creative writing, narrative expansion | Label as fiction or story-world |

Guiding rule:

> Do not flatten the archive. Preserve the versions, label them correctly, and keep the witness protected.
