# UFO to UAP Bridge

**Status:** Public reference note  
**Connected draft:** SOLON Working Draft — May 2026

## Core position

UFO is the cultural and historical archive word. UAP is the modern public-policy and research word. Both matter. Neither should erase the other.

## Use UFO when discussing

- historical sightings and archives,
- older witnesses and community memory,
- UFO Digest and older media lanes,
- civilian researchers and conferences,
- contactee traditions,
- folklore and public cultural history.

## Use UAP when discussing

- modern aviation safety,
- government reporting,
- public policy,
- scientific review,
- sensor data and modern aerospace language,
- contemporary research standards.

## Use UFO/UAP when bridging both worlds

Use UFO/UAP for public pages, book introductions, and association language that must respect both the older witness archive and the newer policy/research vocabulary.

## Public wording

> UFO Association and UAP Associates preserve the older UFO memory field while building a responsible bridge into modern UAP studies, witness care, public education, and research standards.
