## Purpose

UFO Association and UAP Associates exist to preserve witness memory, support public education, organize UFO-to-UAP history, and create a responsible bridge between experiencer testimony, folklore, media history, civic inquiry, and emerging UAP studies.

## The UFO to UAP Bridge

UFO is the cultural and historical archive word. UAP is the modern public-policy and research word. Both matter. Neither should erase the other.

Use **UFO** when preserving historical memory, older witnesses, radio archives, UFO Digest materials, contactee traditions, and cultural lineage.

Use **UAP** when discussing modern policy, aviation safety, government reporting, scientific review, and public-facing research standards.

Use **UFO/UAP** when bridging both worlds.

## Study Lanes

This working draft organizes four public study lanes:

- **Alienology** — TJ Morris cultural-spiritual research language for alien narratives, contact traditions, experiencer accounts, ET symbolism, cosmic identity, and human meaning-making around nonhuman intelligence.
- **Ufology** — the older civilian research and community tradition around sightings, witnesses, investigators, conferences, books, archives, and media.
- **UAP Studies** — the newer civic, scientific, aerospace, defense, and public-policy lane for anomalous aerial phenomena.
- **Experiencer Studies** — the witness-centered lane preserving contact, memory, transformation, meaning, and community care.

## Women in Ufology / UAP Associates

Women have served as witnesses, authors, editors, organizers, archivists, radio hosts, conference builders, researchers, spiritual interpreters, experiencer advocates, and community memory keepers.

UAP Associates can help restore that memory without turning it into gossip, rivalry, or personality conflict.

## Media Memory Lane

The UFO/UAP story is not only sightings. It is also publishing, radio, conferences, websites, books, interviews, witness care, cultural memory, and the people who kept records before the new UAP language became fashionable.

Relevant lanes include UFO Digest, TJ Morris ET Radio, ET Talk TV / TJ Morris Show, ACE Folklife, Hybrid Genies, UFO Association, UAP Associates, and earlier public networks.

## Reality-Layer Standard

This project separates:

- established fact,
- user firsthand memory,
- community / oral history,
- speculative research,
- symbolic / canon language,
- and fiction / worldbuilding.

The rule is simple: do not flatten the archive. Preserve the versions, label them correctly, and protect the witness.

## Working Principle

Build the record. Protect the witness. Separate fact, memory, symbol, speculation, and story. Harm none.
